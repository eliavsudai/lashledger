Lash Ledger

Privacy Policy

Short version: Lash Ledger collects nothing. Your client book lives on your iPhone and nowhere else.

Data Not Collected. Lash Ledger has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We can’t see your clients, maps, photos or earnings — and neither can anyone else.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter — client names and contact details, lash maps, appointment notes, prices, sensitivity notes, patch test dates and settings — is saved in a database inside the app on your iPhone. Before and after photos are copied into the app’s private storage on your iPhone.

None of this is uploaded. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it, unless you have exported a backup.

Photos

Photos you take or choose stay on your phone. They leave the phone only when you share them — for example by tapping Share on a map card or photo, or by exporting a backup — and only to the destination you pick in the iOS share sheet.

Permissions

  • Camera — only to take before and after photos, when you choose “Take Photo”.
  • Photos — only to add pictures you select from your library.
  • Notifications — only if you turn on refill reminders. They are local reminders scheduled on your phone; no push service is used.
  • Face ID — only if you turn on the app lock. Apple handles Face ID; the app never sees your face data.

Backups

“Export backup” creates a single file containing your data and photos and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes (Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, email…) is your choice, and that service’s own privacy terms apply.

Children

Lash Ledger is a business tool for lash professionals and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes
